# Quaternius Ultimate Modular Characters

The characters here (and `Worker.glb`, `Casual_2.glb` in `scripts/blender/quaternius`) are from the Ultimate Modular Men Pack by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com),
released under CC0 1.0 (public domain). They were taken from the GLB conversions in
https://github.com/LuanDucate/Ducz.CharacterCreator (models CC0). `make_driver.py` assembles the driver from
parts of them: the Worker's head without its hard hat, the Casual body, legs and feet, and a cap of our own.
